About

Steady leadership for work’s hardest moments

I’m Adina Dinu - an HR consultant, trainer and coach specialised in psychosocial risks and trauma-informed leadership.

Book a discovery call

I help HR professionals and leaders manage workplace conflict, burnout and crisis with more than just process and compliance.

Work comes with all sorts of challenges. Whether it’s redundancies, workplace investigations, a lack of psychological safety, or employees navigating grief, abuse or illness in their personal lives, leaders and HR teams are asked to deliver results with people who are having a hard time.

My mission is to strengthen individual and collective resilience and capacity by turning traumatic stress from a blind spot into something leaders can prevent, recognise and respond to with confidence and compassion.

My qualifications

Experience, education and professional bodies

  • 20+ years of corporate HR experience
  • M.Sc. in HR Management, B.A. in Psychology
  • Chartered Member of the CIPD, the UK’s leading HR professional body
  • Certified NeuroMindfulness® Coach Practitioner by The NeuroMindfulness Institute
  • Certificate in the Traumatic Stress Studies Certificate Program from the Trauma Research Foundation
  • Member of the UK Psychological Trauma Society and the European Society for Traumatic Stress Studies

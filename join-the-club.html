Why this exists

It doesn’t have to be this hard

Wherever you choose to go, you can get there by yourself. You probably have the smarts, the willpower, the tools and insights, and at least one safe person in your life you can talk to, otherwise you wouldn’t be here.

But everything gets quicker in good company. No single book or therapist can know it all. The power of a group of people who know exactly what you mean because they’ve lived it too never ceases to amaze me. That collective, hard-won wisdom is worth more than any one voice.

Also, the spaces where we can just be ourselves and grow — unsure, unpolished, asking for help, practising what we’re not good at yet — keep shrinking as we become the people others depend on. So we are building one - a place where we can drop the mask and get on with the work, together.

Is this for you?

What you should know before you join

This is for you if…

  • You’re not in any danger or crisis
  • You’re rebuilding or growing after difficult experiences at work or in your personal life
  • You’ve been diagnosed with PTSD/cPTSD and you’re figuring out how it may affect your work
  • You feel well enough to function, e.g. at work, as a parent etc.

This is NOT for you if…

  • You often feel very sad, agitated, overwhelmed, or numb
  • You get frequent flashbacks, or spend a lot of time thinking about the past
  • You feel cut off from your life and the people around you
  • Some days you’re unable to carry out simple, routine activities.
If this is you, please seek support from your GP or a mental health professional. For anyone living in the UK, here’s how to get support for a mental health emergency: Where to get urgent help for mental health – NHS.

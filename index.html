Hello, I’m Adina

A trauma-informed coach, speaker, trainer, and HR consultant.

Before setting up my own business, I spent 20+ years in recruitment, talent, and leadership development with some of the biggest brands in the world.

Through both my work and a personal experience of building a career while recovering from trauma, I’ve seen firsthand how unrecognised psychological harm can last for years and affect careers, relationships, wellbeing, team effectiveness, and business outcomes. I’ve also discovered that HR teams and team leaders rarely have the awareness and capabilities they need to spot psychosocial risks and injury, and take effective action.

That’s what I do today. I help HR and team leaders lay healthy foundations, on which psychological safety, resilience and performance can be built to last.

Steady leadership for work’s hardest moments

Turning traumatic stress from a blind spot into something leaders can prevent, recognise and respond to.

Work comes with all sorts of challenges. Whether it’s redundancies, workplace investigations, a lack of psychological safety, or employees navigating grief, abuse or illness in their personal lives, leaders and HR teams are asked to deliver results with people who are having a hard time.

My mission is to strengthen individual and collective resilience and capacity by turning traumatic stress from a blind spot into something leaders can prevent, recognise and respond to with confidence and compassion.

  • Redundancies
  • Workplace investigations
  • Psychological safety
  • Grief, abuse or illness
